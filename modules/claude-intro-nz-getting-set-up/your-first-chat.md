# Make a first draft and check it

Allow 2 minutes.

Open a new chat in your approved account. Use this invented example. You can copy the prompt below.

```text
Draft an email for an invented Hamilton accountancy.
Audience: a client coming to an appointment.
Ask them to bring last year's accounts and a list of questions.
Use NZ English, a warm tone, and fewer than 90 words.
Include a subject line. Use [appointment date] as a placeholder.
Do not add deadlines, fees, tax advice or extra document requests.
```

## Review the result

Look for the two requested items. Check that Claude kept the placeholder and did not invent a deadline. Read the tone aloud. Would you be comfortable sending it after review?

If the result needs work, send a follow-up: "Make the email more direct. Keep both requested items. Keep the date placeholder."

Save the reviewed practice draft in your own notes.

## Ready for the quiz

You should now know your account type, one privacy setting to check, and why every draft needs review. The five-question quiz asks you to apply those choices. Aim for four correct answers.
