# Choose an account and understand the plans

Allow 3 minutes.

Use your employer's approved Claude account for work. If your employer provides an organisation workspace, ask which workspace to use. A work email address alone does not make a personal subscription a business workspace.

## What the plan names mean

| Plan | Practical meaning for a NZ business |
| --- | --- |
| Free | A place to practise. It includes chat, files and chat-created Artifacts. You can create up to five Projects. |
| Pro | An individual subscription with more usage than Free. |
| Max | An individual subscription with a choice of higher usage tiers. |
| Team | An organisation plan with central administration and billing. It needs at least two members. |
| Enterprise | An organisation plan with additional controls, such as audit logs and custom data retention. |

Free, Pro and Max are personal plans. Team and Enterprise use commercial data terms. More usage does not remove the need for approval to use business information.

## Set up for the exercises

Open [claude.ai](https://claude.ai/) and follow its sign-in or account creation prompts. You do not need to buy a plan for this course. An approved Free account and invented examples are enough.

Free and paid plans can have usage limits. Paying does not guarantee unlimited use. If Claude displays a limit, follow the reset information in the account. Save your exercise notes and continue when access returns. Do not budget on a fixed number of messages per day.

## Quick decision

Write your plan name and whether it is a personal account or an organisation workspace. Ask your manager which account your team approves before using real work data.
