# Check privacy before your first work chat

Allow 5 minutes.

Use invented information for every exercise. Make this check before you type a work prompt.

## Personal accounts: Free, Pro and Max

1. Select your name and open **Settings**.
2. Open **Privacy**.
3. Find **Help Improve our AI models** and switch it **off** for these exercises.

This setting controls model improvement. Anthropic still describes safety-review exceptions. Turning it off does not create a confidential business agreement or remove all storage.

## Organisation accounts: Team and Enterprise

Anthropic does not train on commercial inputs and outputs by default. Feedback or an explicit opt-in can be exceptions. Ask your account owner about the organisation's data rules and feedback controls. Do not submit a work conversation with thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback unless your policy allows it.

## A temporary chat is still processed

Incognito chats are not saved to chat history or memory and are not used for training. They are retained for 30 days by default; an organisation's retention setting can mean longer storage. Organisation owners can access them through exports. Incognito is not permission to enter private data.

## Try it: five-minute check

Find the Privacy page in your approved account. Record only the setting name and whether it is off. Do not copy account identifiers or take a screenshot of private account details.

Next, write a list of three things you will keep out of practice chats: for example, a client's IRD number, an employee's medical details, and a password.

> **Warning:** A privacy setting does not replace your employer's approval or your obligations under the Privacy Act 2020.
