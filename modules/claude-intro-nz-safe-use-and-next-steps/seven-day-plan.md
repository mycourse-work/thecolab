# Your seven-day plan and further training

Allow 5 minutes.

Choose one task you can check. Spend five minutes a day practising with invented or public information. Keep your reviewed outputs and useful prompts in your own notes.

## Build a small habit

| Day | Five-minute task | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm your approved account and privacy setting. | A short setup note. |
| 2 | Draft the Hamilton appointment reminder. | A reviewed email. |
| 3 | Improve the builder's quote update prompt. | The before and after prompts. |
| 4 | Create a private practice Project. | Instructions you have checked. |
| 5 | Summarise the invented quote-notes file. | A table checked against its source. |
| 6 | Revise an induction checklist Artifact or chat draft. | One specific improvement. |
| 7 | Propose team rules and pick your next task. | An owner and a next action. |

## Choose support when you need it

Keep practising for free if that meets your needs. When you want more structure, [browse Claude Training NZ's courses](https://claudetraining.co.nz/courses/). Online courses start at **NZ$199 + GST**. Check the current options with TheColab.

For guided team practice, explore the [Claude foundations half-day workshop](https://claudetraining.co.nz/courses/claude-foundations-workshop/), available in person or remotely. For deeper work, see [Claude for everyday work](https://claudetraining.co.nz/courses/claude-practical-day/), [finance teams](https://claudetraining.co.nz/courses/claude-for-finance-teams/), or [HR and people teams](https://claudetraining.co.nz/courses/claude-for-hr-and-people-teams/). Choose the course that matches the work you do.

## Finish the course

Complete each lesson and pass all four quizzes. The pass mark is 80%: four out of five questions in each quiz. The platform has a course-completion certificate for this tenant. Use **Get Your Certificate** when it becomes available after your recorded completion. Your progress must be associated with an account.

This is a TheColab course-completion certificate. It is not an Anthropic certification. Take your seven-day plan into your next week of work.
