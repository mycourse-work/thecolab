# ChatGPT at work: start here

This free course gives you a practical way to start using ChatGPT in a New Zealand business. Allow about **83 minutes**, including exercises and four short quizzes. You can pause between modules.

**TheColab provides independent training. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of their respective owners.**

## What you will make

- A clear email draft and quote cover note.
- Meeting actions that keep unknown details visible.
- A small data summary with numbers you check yourself.
- A simple team AI policy starter and a seven-day practice plan.

## What you need

Use a web browser and an approved ChatGPT account. A Free account is enough for the core practice. If an optional feature is missing or you reach a limit, use the text alternative in that lesson. Ask your manager which account is approved for work.

Every business scenario is invented for learning. The Hamilton accountancy, Christchurch builder, and Wellington HR team are illustrative examples, not customers or testimonials. Use the supplied fictional material. Do not use real client or staff records.
