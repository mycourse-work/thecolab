# Welcome to Microsoft 365 Copilot: a free introduction

Make a useful draft. Check it. Keep control of the work.

This free course is for New Zealand office staff, owners and managers. Allow **84 minutes**, including short exercises and four quizzes. You need no technical background.

## What you will leave with

- A clear view of the Copilot experience on your account.
- A reusable prompt for an email, quote cover note or summary.
- A way to check a Word draft, workbook answer or meeting action.
- A draft team AI rule and a seven-day practice plan.

## How to take the course

Work through four modules in order. Each starts with a short video, then has three to five text lessons and a quiz. If a video is not yet available, read its summary and continue. You can complete every core exercise with the invented text in the lessons. You do not need to buy a Copilot add-on for this course.

For hands-on practice, use an employer-approved work account with Copilot Chat. If you have no suitable account, write your prompts and checks on paper. The app lessons also offer a Chat route. Buttons and access vary by licence, app version and your organisation's settings.

> **Important:** All businesses, people and work records in the exercises are invented illustrations. They are not customers or testimonials. Use these examples before you use real work information.

## Finish and record your learning

Mark each lesson complete and pass all four quizzes. The course pass mark is 80%. Read the feedback and try again if needed. Sign in to save progress. This tenant supports an end-of-course certificate for eligible signed-in learners after completion. The certificate records this independent course; it is not a Microsoft qualification.
