# Welcome to Claude for NZ work

Start with one small task you can check. This free course helps NZ office staff, owners and managers use Claude for practical drafting and document work.

## What you will do

1. Set up an approved account and check privacy settings.
2. Write clear prompts with context, examples and limits.
3. Use Projects, files and Artifacts for repeat work.
4. Protect information, check answers and make a seven-day plan.

Allow about 84 minutes 13 seconds, plus this two-minute welcome. Each of the four modules starts with a short video, then has four short text lessons and a five-question quiz. The text summary also lets you learn when a video is unavailable. Work at your own pace. Allow up to five minutes for each exercise.

## What you need

Use a browser and your employer's approved Claude account. You can complete the practice tasks on Free. Use only invented or public information. If an account or capability is unavailable, read the example and write the reviewed result in your own notes.

All businesses, staff roles and situations in the exercises are invented illustrations. They are not customers or testimonials. Links in the lessons take you to the official guidance. Check current availability before using a feature.

## Completion

Mark the lessons complete and pass all four quizzes at 80%: four out of five questions. You can retry after reviewing the feedback. Use the platform's course-completion certificate once your account has recorded the required completion.
